Our Mission

Our mission as Duru Agro Food Machinery is to manufacture durable, high-efficiency machines customized for each food processing line, to increase the facility's efficiency without compromising food safety, and to provide seamless technical support from installation onward.

What We Commit to in Our Machines

  • Sizing per facility: design based on raw material, capacity and line layout.
  • Food-grade components: stainless steel bodies, food-grade belts and transfer parts.
  • Working mechanisms: structures that make adjustment, cleaning and maintenance easy.
  • Documentation and training: assembly, operating instructions and operator training.

How We Deliver It

Before production, we define the operating conditions together: raw material characteristics, daily volume, position in the line, staff size. Based on these parameters we adjust dimensions, motor power and capacity; the machine is installed at the facility, tested, and handed over after the personnel training.

Our Benchmark

Success is measured by the machine running without problems in the facility. To reach that benchmark in every delivery, we develop the design, production and commissioning process together with the facility's operational data.
