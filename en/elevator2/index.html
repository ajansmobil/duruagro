Elevator Model 2 — Second Transfer Point in the Line

Elevator Model 2 is a belt conveyor machine designed for the second transfer point in the line. It transfers the product to the next unit (the roasting or cooling point) with a stable flow.

Introduction

Its in-line position connects the outlet of the previous unit to the inlet of the next unit. Belt speed, flow and level difference are set per project. A spill tray and a dust-tight cover are recommended at the belt end.

Features

  • Food-grade belt, stainless steel body.
  • Gear-motor option for flow adjustment.
  • Easy access to wear belts and bearings.
  • Safety circuit, emergency stop.

Applications

  • Transfer from the screening/cleaning outlet to the roasting unit.
  • Product feeding to the cooling point.
  • Intermediate transport in various food lines.
